[LEADERSHIP LAB: The Craft of Writing Effectively](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vtIzMaLkCaM)
> How to write *academically*. Is quite aggressive view. But I feel it fits in academic writing.

- NOT sharing but CHANGING others' thoughts.
- writing FOR the reader. instead of JUST to convey an idea.
- function of writing: to move conversation forward. Not to stay in drawer.
- There're donimate ideas in the field. Why should your different opinion be accepted.
  - acknowledge a community face this problem
  - know code to talk -> acknowledge previous work
  - Give what's wrong. -> do not explain, do it in a way that you've anticipated the question.
- Steps to write.
  - Problem <- what readers care about. Use instable words: abnormal, inconsistency.
  - Solution <- only when problem is acknowledged.
  - Literature Review -> enrich the problems. e.g. from xxx, his idea complicates the whole problem, he said xxx in later year, which even make it complex. background, history, etc, *BUT*, afterwards, brings up your understanding of the problem
