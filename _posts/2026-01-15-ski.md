## Transportation


- [Car park](https://www.avoriaz.com/en/staying/access-and-transport/car-parks/): €80/week + €18/day for outdoor. 100/week + sth for indoor (need reservation).
- [Town plan map](https://www.calameo.com/avoriaz/read/002827892871be4c28999) to see where the car park is.
- ~~[Book Altibus](https://www.altibus.com/en/top-destinations/morzine-avoriaz/): €49*2 for return from/to Geneva.~~
- [Within town transportation](https://www.avoriaz.com/sejourner/acces-et-transport/transports-sur-place/), use either horse sleigh (No reservation required but also I can't read the price sheet); Or Caterpillar fares: Single fare of €13.50 with and without luggage for 1 to 5 people + €2 per additional person
- Free [Shuttle bus schedule](https://www.calameo.com/avoriaz/read/0028278922434dec8531c?page=1) to other town and [realtime track](https://app.pysae.com/screen/#/skibus_morzine-avoriaz/all).

- **Driving** time Amsterdam > GVA
  - Amsterdam > Maastricht 2h - 2h30 213km
  - Maastricht A
    - Maastricht > Bazel BSL 5h - 5h30
      - 521km via Luxemburg, Nancy (cheap fuel)
      - 511km via Saarbrucken (get out of Belgium faster)
      - 556km via Koblenz/A61 (flatter, most time in Germany)
    -  Bazel BSL > Geneva GVA 2h30 - 3h 255km
  -  Maastricht B
      -  Maastricht > Geneva GVA 7h - 8h 700km via Luxemburg, Dijon
  -  Realistically leave around 01:00 from Amsterdam to be in Geneva around 11:00
  -  Tank in Luxemburg, France (Switzerland very expensive)

- **Flight**
  - if arrive by flight, pls book as early as possible. it'll be best to collect skis the day before. ski shop close around 18:30. geneva to place driving is about 2h+?. so plan accordingly.

  - P2 short term parking

  - 1h40 to resort

    - begins on the first day at midnight and ends on the sixth or seventh day at midnight (which midnight? do we need extra day?)
