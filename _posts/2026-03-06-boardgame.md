# Lords of xidirs [>>](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/156566/lords-of-xidit)

- Overall ![stars](https://starrating-beta.vercel.app/3/)
- Length of play: 2~3h for 5p game.
- It'll help a looot if you have good memory of what other players took and had. Otherwise it's difficult to make optimal moves or even, waste a lot of steps.
- Played on `2026-04-17`. ![Setups](/assets/images/bg_xidir.jpeg)

# Cascadian [>>](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/295947/cascadia)
> Play on [Web](https://cascadiagame.github.io/) or [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2438970/Cascadia/)

- Overall ![stars](https://starrating-beta.vercel.app/4.0/)
- Length of play: ~1h for 4p game.
- We used the setup for Bear-D, Elk-, Salmon, Halk-C, Fox-. Halk is very point efficient in this setup if you can manage to get a hexgen of 7 halks.
- Played on `2026-03-06`. ![Results](/assets/images/bg_cascadian.jpg)


# Wingspan
> Play on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1993180/Quilts_and_Cats_of_Calico/)
- Overall ![stars](https://starrating-beta.vercel.app/4.5/)
- Balance the birds. But fill woods and water first is better strategy for me. Try to get birds that lay eggs when other players lay eggs. Also like bird that you can move to other habitate if it's on most right.
