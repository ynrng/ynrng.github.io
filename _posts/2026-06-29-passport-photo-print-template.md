Use this [Google Slide](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1jXH2ebnW8lvbtNhLyk5kRO8r1CxVp4Fxv6l8QQT1l00/edit?slide=id.g3efbac2c2d2_0_61#slide=id.g3efbac2c2d2_0_61) to export photos. Guidelines are also provided in slide.

1. Check what lines and boxes means in the template.

2. Fit your photo ( white background ) within all the lines and boxes in 2nd slide. You can remove background naturally with iPhone Photo app.

3. Choose "Export to pdf" as the direct export as jpeg doesn't give as good quality picture as you want. Then use your own system pdf tool to export to maximum quality jpeg.

4. Go to boots store (It supports phone transfer and usb etc) or order online. Select 6*4 print. Check stores and instructions [here](https://www.bootsphoto.com/photo-printing/instant-photo-prints.html)
