## Different Pedestrian crossings

### Non signal controlled crossings
#### [Zebra and parallel crossings](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/using-the-road-159-to-203#rule195)
![alt text](https://assets.digital.cabinet-office.gov.uk/media/55886772e5274a1573000016/the-highway-code-rule-19.jpg)
#### Parallel crossings = zebra + cycle route alongside.

### Signal-controlled crossings

#### [Pelican crossings](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/using-the-road-159-to-203#rule196)

![Pelican](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d4/Pelikan_Walvis_Bay.jpg/960px-Pelikan_Walvis_Bay.jpg)

These are signal-controlled crossings where flashing amber follows the red ‘Stop’ light. You MUST stop when the red light shows. When the amber light is flashing, you MUST give way to any pedestrians on the crossing. If the amber light is flashing and there are no pedestrians on the crossing, you may proceed with caution.

\__PHASING OUT by puffins.\__

#### [Puffin crossings](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/using-the-road-159-to-203#rule199)

![Puffin](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/aa/Papageitaucher_Fratercula_arctica.jpg/2560px-Papageitaucher_Fratercula_arctica.jpg)

These are similar to pelican crossings, but there is no flashing amber phase;

## 1. Road Stud Colours (Cat's Eyes)

![Motorway Stud Colours](https://theorytest.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/motorway-studs.jpg)

| Stud Colour |  meaning
| --- | ---
| White | Marks lane lines or centre of single-carriageway roads.
| Red | Marks the left edge of the road (hard shoulder/verge).
| Amber | Marks the right edge next to the central reservation.
| Green | Marks slip roads, lay-bys, and permitted joining/leaving points.
| Green/Yellow (Temporary) | Used in roadworks or contraflow systems.

## National Speed Limits (Cars & Motorcycles)

|Road Type                       | Speed Limit|
|--------------------------------| -------------|
|Built-up areas (street lights)  | **30 mph**|
|Single carriageways             | **60 mph**|
|Dual carriageways               | **70 mph**|
|Motorways                       | **70 mph**|
| Towing | -10mph |

**Wales:** 20 mph default on restricted roads.

|--------------------------------| -------------|

##  Vehicle Light Colours & Meanings

Colour | Used by
--- | ---
Blue | Emergency vehicles responding (police, ambulance, fire, etc.).
Red | Used by emergency vehicles when stopped; "do not pass" at scenes.
Amber/Yellow | Slow-moving or hazardous vehicles (breakdown trucks, tractors, gritters,roadwork vehicles).
Green | Doctors or paramedics on call.
White | Headlights, reversing lights.
Purple (rare) | Funeral convoys.
